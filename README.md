# SailabAI

A flood digital twin for the Chenab, Ravi and Sutlej rivers around Multan. It maps today's flood
from Sentinel-1 radar (Model 1) and forecasts the flood chance for every pixel at the next
satellite pass and +1, +2, +3, +5 and +7 days (Model 2), with honest confidence levels and risk
down to roads and buildings.

> **Experimental research output, not an official flood warning.** SailabAI is a final-year
> project for flood experts and planners (NDMA, PDMA, relief groups), not the general public.

Full documentation is being written; see `docs/` as it lands.
